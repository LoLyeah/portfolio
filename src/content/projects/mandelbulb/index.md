**Mandelbulb** is an interactive mathematical visualizer that renders stunning 3D representations of the Mandelbulb fractal directly in your browser. Utilizing high-performance rendering techniques, it lets users explore the mesmerizing complexity of fractal geometry.

### Key Features
- **Real-Time Rendering:** Highly optimized raymarching algorithms for smooth 3D navigation and zooming.
- **Custom Parameter Controls:** Tune formulas, power values, iteration depths, and color palettes on the fly.
- **Stunning Aesthetics:** Deep HSL-based lighting, soft shadows, and glassmorphism styling for controls.
- **Educational Resource:** Visually inspect complex number geometry and spatial coordinates in real-time.
