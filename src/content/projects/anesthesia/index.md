**Anesthesia** is a state-of-the-art, premium clinical assistant suite designed for medical doctors and healthcare professionals. Built on **Next.js 16** with a dark-mode glassmorphic theme, it serves as an offline-capable hub for medical analysis, physical exams, clinical guidelines, and AI consultations.

### Key Features
- 🧠 **AI-Powered Consultations:** Symptom analysis, drug interaction checking, and clinical decisions using Gemini, GROQ, and OpenAI, beautifully rendered in markdown.
- 🧍 **Interactive 2D Body Selector Map:** Clickable anatomy locator to find physical exam guidelines matched with AI-driven documentation sync.
- 📄 **PDF Clinical Report Export:** Instant download of formatted PDFs for symptom reports or drug charts to facilitate patient documentation.
- 📋 **Structured Specialty Guidelines (CPG):** Specialty-structured (Psychiatry, Orthopedics, Interventional Radiology) offline guidelines with hyperlinking and query fallback.
- 🔄 **Intelligent AI Data Sync:** Automated data gathering pipeline with side-by-side diffing and strict human-in-the-loop validation.
- 🧮 **Context-Aware Medical Calculators:** Dosing, BMI, and Creatinine Clearance calculators.
