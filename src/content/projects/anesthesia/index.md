**Anesthesia** is a highly specialized medical calculator and monitoring assistant built entirely in TypeScript. It assists healthcare professionals in instantly calculating accurate drug dosages, infusion rates, and patient-specific physiological markers.

### Key Features
- **Precise Dosage Calculators:** Multi-variable equations supporting a wide range of standard clinical agents.
- **Physiological Tuning:** Automatic adjustments based on patient parameters (age, weight, and status).
- **TypeScript Core:** Solid type safety and highly robust mathematical modules.
- **Aesthetic UI:** Minimalist and high-contrast design optimized for clinical settings.
